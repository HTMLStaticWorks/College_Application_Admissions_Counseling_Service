Our story

Started on the other side of the admissions desk

Campusly began in 2011 when Dr. Elena Rodriguez left the University of Pennsylvania admissions office. After a decade of reading files, she kept seeing the same thing: talented students whose applications didn’t show who they really were, simply because no one had explained how the process works.

She started counseling a handful of local families from a small Palo Alto office. Word spread, and colleagues from Stanford, Columbia and MIT joined her. Today our team of former admissions officers supports families across 30+ countries, yet each counselor still takes only 12 new students per cycle.

Our Counseling Philosophy

Four principles guide every conversation, every essay edit and every school list we build.

01

Your voice, not ours

We ask questions and push for honesty, but we never write your essays. Committees can tell, and so can you.

02

Fit before prestige

A balanced list of schools where you will thrive beats a list of famous names chosen by ranking.

03

Honest odds

We tell families what a committee would actually think, even when it’s not what they hoped to hear.

04

Less stress, more ownership

Clear timelines and small weekly goals let students own the process and keep the dinner table calm.
