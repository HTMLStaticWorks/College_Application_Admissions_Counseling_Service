Essay Coaching

Your voice, refined and elevated for admissions committees.

The personal statement is your opportunity to become more than just a GPA and test score. We guide you through our proven narrative discovery process.

  • Brainstorming: Uncovering your core narrative arc.
  • Outlining: Structuring the essay for maximum impact.
  • Drafting & Revision: Unlimited feedback rounds until perfection.

Strategic School Selection

Building a balanced, data-driven college list.

We leverage historical admissions data and our deep understanding of institutional priorities to help you build a strategic list of Reach, Target, and Safety schools.

  • Academic Assessment: Honest evaluation of your profile.
  • Fit Analysis: Matching you with campus cultures where you'll thrive.
  • ED/EA Strategy: Optimizing early application rounds to boost odds.

Interview Preparation

Turn anxiety into authentic confidence.

Alumni and admissions interviews can be intimidating. We conduct rigorous mock interviews to help you articulate your passions and handle unexpected questions.

  • Mock Sessions: Realistic simulations with former interviewers.
  • Feedback: Detailed rubrics on body language and content.
  • Question Bank: Access to hundreds of real past interview questions.
